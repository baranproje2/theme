(08) — FAQ

Good questions.

Is there a minimum order?

12 pieces for embroidery and 48 pieces for screen printing.

How long does it take?

Seven to ten working days once your artwork is approved. Rush requests are welcome — just ask.

Will I see my design before it's made?

Absolutely. Every order goes through a final artwork approval before production starts.

How should I send my artwork?

Email it to info@pacapparel.com as a JPEG or EPS file.

Are there setup fees?

Screen printing has a one-time setup fee for screens and films; embroidery has a one-time digitizing fee. Both depend on your design — send your logo for an exact quote.

How does payment work?

50% deposit when the order is placed, the balance at pickup or delivery. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and company checks.
